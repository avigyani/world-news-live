Key points

  • 49th ASEAN Summit runs 15–17 November in Manila, chaired by the Philippines.
  • Modi arrives Monday for the ASEAN-India and East Asia summits; Trump arrives Tuesday for a special ASEAN-US summit.
  • Agenda includes Myanmar, a South China Sea code of conduct, trade and maritime security.
  • A Quad leaders' meeting on the sidelines is expected but not yet officially confirmed.
