Trump, Xi and Putin Set for Historic Meeting in Shenzhen as Nuclear Arms and Iran Top the Agenda

November 14, 2026 · World News Live brief · Source: Times Now

The leaders of the world's three largest nuclear powers are set to meet in Shenzhen next week, with Iran and nuclear arms on the agenda — though officials say no deals are expected.

Photo: Fumikas Sagisavas / Wikimedia Commons (CC0).

The leaders of the United States, China and Russia plan to meet in Shenzhen next week on the sidelines of the APEC summit, in what would be an unprecedented gathering of the world's biggest nuclear powers, reports said.

US President Donald Trump has said he wants to use the meeting to discuss the US war with Iran and push for a three-way agreement on nuclear weapons. A senior US official told reporters that Trump would raise Iran with both Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin, but planned to avoid the subject of Ukraine.

The nuclear question has grown more pressing since New START, the last arms treaty between Washington and Moscow, expired in February, leaving no limits on the two largest nuclear arsenals for the first time in more than 50 years.

Experts cautioned that the talks were likely to offer more show than substance, with the official saying no agreements were expected to be announced. Ukraine's President Volodymyr Zelenskyy warned the summit could become a place to discuss a Ukraine deal without Kyiv.

Key points

  • Trump, Xi and Putin to meet in Shenzhen on the APEC sidelines
  • Iran and a three-way nuclear deal on Trump's agenda
  • New START expired in February — no limits on the two largest arsenals
  • No agreements expected, a senior US official said
  • Zelenskyy warned against a Ukraine deal discussed without Kyiv
