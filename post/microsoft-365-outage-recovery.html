Microsoft 365 Outage Enters Second Day as Outlook, Teams and Copilot Slowly Recover

November 14, 2026 · World News Live brief · Source: The Hindu

Microsoft said services were gradually recovering on Friday after a global outage knocked out Outlook, Teams and Copilot for nearly two days, with no firm time given for full recovery.

AI-generated illustration for this story.

A major Microsoft 365 outage stretched into a second day on Friday, disrupting Outlook, Teams and Copilot for millions of users, companies and government offices around the world, reports said.

The disruption began before 11:17 am ET on Thursday, according to outage tracker Downdetector, which logged almost 20,000 reports a minute at the peak and about 140,000 Outlook reports from 74 countries on Thursday afternoon. Staff at the US House of Representatives were among those affected.

Microsoft said a portion of its infrastructure was experiencing elevated load across several regions, and that recovery actions were progressing in phases. The company extended the email timeout window to 48 hours to preserve delayed messages while mail queues were cleared.

The company apologised and said there was no indication the outage was caused by malicious or cyber activity. It added that it would publish a full post-incident report with the root cause once mitigation was complete, but had not given a time for full recovery.

Key points

  • Outage began on Thursday and hit Outlook, Teams, Copilot and more
  • About 140,000 Outlook reports came in from 74 countries on Thursday
  • Microsoft blamed elevated load on part of its infrastructure
  • No evidence of a cyberattack, the company said
  • No ETA yet for full recovery
