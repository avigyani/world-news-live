Bay of Bengal Depression Brings Heavy Rain Warning for Tamil Nadu and Puducherry

November 13, 2026 · World News Live brief · Source: The Hindu BusinessLine

A depression over the southwest Bay of Bengal is expected to bring heavy rainfall to coastal Tamil Nadu and Puducherry, the India Meteorological Department has said.

AI-generated illustration for this story.

A depression has formed over the southwest Bay of Bengal, and the India Meteorological Department (IMD) has forecast heavy rainfall for coastal Tamil Nadu and Puducherry as the system moves.

The system is being tracked as it approaches the coast, and the IMD is issuing regular updates on its intensity and likely path. Coastal districts are expected to see the heaviest showers.

The northeast monsoon, which runs from October to December, is the main rainy season for Tamil Nadu and Puducherry. Depressions forming in the Bay of Bengal during this period often bring intense, localised downpours.

Residents in coastal areas have been advised to follow official weather updates, and the situation is being monitored as the system develops.

Key points

  • A depression has formed over the southwest Bay of Bengal.
  • The IMD forecasts heavy rainfall for coastal Tamil Nadu and Puducherry.
  • The northeast monsoon (October–December) is the region's main rainy season.
  • The IMD is issuing regular updates as the system moves toward the coast.
