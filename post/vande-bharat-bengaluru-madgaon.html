PM Modi to Flag Off Bengaluru-Mangaluru-Madgaon Vande Bharat Express

November 13, 2026 · World News Live brief · Source: Indian Express

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will inaugurate the new semi-high-speed train on November 13, improving rail connectivity between Bengaluru, coastal Karnataka and Goa.

AI-generated illustration for this story.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will inaugurate the Bengaluru-Mangaluru-Madgaon Vande Bharat Express on November 13, a new semi-high-speed service aimed at improving rail connectivity between Bengaluru, coastal Karnataka and Goa.

The service is expected to cut travel time on a route that links Karnataka's capital with its coast and onward to Goa, one of the busiest leisure and business corridors in the region.

The inauguration comes ahead of a busy fortnight in the capital: the 45th India International Trade Fair opens at Bharat Mandapam in New Delhi on November 14 and runs to November 27 on the theme 'Vikas aur Virasat'.

Key points

  • Inauguration on November 13 by PM Modi
  • Connects Bengaluru with Mangaluru and Madgaon (Goa)
  • India International Trade Fair opens Nov 14 in New Delhi
