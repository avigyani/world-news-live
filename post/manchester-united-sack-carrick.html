Manchester United Sack Michael Carrick After Worst Start in Decades

November 13, 2026 · World News Live brief · Source: The Hindu

Michael Carrick was fired on Thursday after overseeing the club's worst start to a Premier League season, with the team 18th in the table and one league win to its name.

AI-generated illustration for this story.

Manchester United sacked head coach Michael Carrick on Thursday, November 12, after a run that left the 20-time English champions with their worst start to a Premier League season, and their worst start to a league campaign since 1986-87.

United sit 18th in the table with a single league win, and next travel to Liverpool. US men's national team coach Mauricio Pochettino is reported to be close to the job, with a verbal agreement said to be in place pending his release from US Soccer.

The decision ends Carrick's tenure at a club where he previously served as a player and coach, and opens a search that will shape the rest of a season already in crisis.

Key points

  • Carrick sacked on Nov 12 after the club's worst start since 1986-87
  • United 18th with one league win; Liverpool away next
  • Pochettino reportedly close, pending US Soccer release
