Cabinet Clears Rs 62,370 Crore Fertiliser Subsidy as Moody's Lifts India Growth Forecast to 7%

November 13, 2026 · World News Live brief · Source: The Hindu BusinessLine

The Union Cabinet approved a fertiliser subsidy package 64 percent larger than last year's for the Rabi season, while Moody's raised India's FY27 growth forecast and flagged inflation risks.

AI-generated illustration for this story.

The Union Cabinet has approved a Rs 62,370-crore fertiliser subsidy package for the Rabi 2026-27 season, a 64 percent increase over last year, aimed at supporting farmers through the winter crop season.

Moody's Ratings raised India's FY27 growth forecast to 7 percent from 6.5 percent, citing strong economic momentum and resilience, while cautioning about high inflation.

In a separate decision, the Cabinet approved a Rs 3,230-crore project to transform the historic North and South Block buildings in New Delhi into the Yuge Yugeen Bharat National Museum, billed as the world's largest museum. Meanwhile, the India Meteorological Department upgraded a developing system over the south-east Bay of Bengal, with heavy rain likely in Tamil Nadu and Puducherry.

Key points

  • Fertiliser subsidy Rs 62,370 crore for Rabi 2026-27, up 64%
  • Moody's raises FY27 growth forecast to 7%, flags inflation
  • Rs 3,230 crore museum project for North and South Block approved
  • Bay of Bengal system upgraded; heavy rain likely in Tamil Nadu, Puducherry
