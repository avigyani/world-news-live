Salman Khan Wishes Shraddha Kapoor's Eetha Ahead of December 4 Box Office Clash

November 13, 2026 · World News Live brief · Source: Times Now

Salman Khan extended his best wishes to the team of Eetha on social media, even as his own film Maatrubhumi is set to release on the same day, December 4, 2026.

AI-generated illustration for this story.

Salman Khan took to Instagram Stories on November 12 to extend his best wishes to the team of Eetha, starring Shraddha Kapoor, drawing attention because the two films share a release date.

Maatrubhumi: May War Rest in Peace, starring Salman Khan and Chitrangda Singh and directed by Apoorva Lakhia, is a war drama inspired by the real-life clash between Indian and Chinese troops in the Galwan Valley. Its trailer released on November 10.

Eetha, directed by Laxman Utekar, is a musical drama starring Shraddha Kapoor and Randeep Hooda, inspired by the life of legendary Marathi Tamasha and Lavani performer Vithabai Narayangaonkar. Both films arrive in theatres on December 4, 2026, setting up one of the year's most watched box office clashes.

Key points

  • Salman wishes the Eetha team on Instagram Stories
  • Both films release December 4, 2026
  • Maatrubhumi: Galwan-inspired war drama; Eetha: biopic of Vithabai Narayangaonkar
