November 21, 2026 · World News Live brief · Source: The Economic Times

Key points

  • NSE's market value reached about $59.65 billion, passing Nasdaq ($57.6 billion) and HKEX ($57.38 billion).
  • Shares rose 2.8% to an intraday high of Rs 2,251, and are up about 26% in November since the September 24 listing.
  • NSE is now India's ninth-largest listed company by market value.
  • CME Group leads the global ranking at about $111 billion, followed by Intercontinental Exchange at about $95 billion.
