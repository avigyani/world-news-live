India U19s Storm Into Asia Cup Final After Crushing Afghanistan; Pakistan Await on Saturday

November 13, 2026 · World News Live brief · Source: JagranJosh

India won their semi-final by eight wickets in Oman and will meet arch-rivals Pakistan in the final on 14 November.

AI-generated illustration for this story.

India defeated Afghanistan by eight wickets in the semi-final of the ACC Men's U19 Asia Cup 2026 in Oman, booking a place in Saturday's final against Pakistan.

The semi-final was played in Al Amerat, Oman, where India sealed the win with eight wickets to spare to set up the title clash. The result keeps India on course for the continental crown in a tournament that showcases the best young cricketers from across Asia.

The final, on Saturday, 14 November, sets up one of youth cricket's most watched rivalries, with India and Pakistan meeting for the continental crown. Both sides won their semi-finals to reach the decider, and the match is expected to draw a large television audience in both countries.

India and Pakistan have a long rivalry at every level of the game, and youth finals between the two sides are among the most watched fixtures in the age-group calendar.

Key points

  • India beat Afghanistan by eight wickets in the semi-final
  • Final against Pakistan scheduled for Saturday, 14 November
  • Semi-final played at Al Amerat in Oman
  • India and Pakistan meet for the U19 Asia Cup title
