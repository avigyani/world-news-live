US Envoys Meet Putin in Moscow After Abu Dhabi Talks Agree Prisoner Swap

November 13, 2026 · World News Live brief · Source: Open Chronicle

Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner arrived in Moscow for talks with Vladimir Putin, a day after two days of US-mediated negotiations in Abu Dhabi produced agreement on a prisoner exchange but no breakthrough on a ceasefire or territory.

AI-generated illustration for this story.

American envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner arrived in Moscow on Thursday for discussions with Russian President Vladimir Putin, following two days of negotiations involving American, Russian and Ukrainian representatives in Abu Dhabi.

The Abu Dhabi talks, the first US-brokered round since February, ended with the two sides agreeing in principle to a large prisoner exchange. Questions of a ceasefire and territory remain deadlocked, according to reports of the discussions.

The diplomacy played out against continued strikes. Russian attacks on Ukraine's energy grid left more than 850,000 consumers without power across the Dnipropetrovsk and Kharkiv regions, energy operator DTEK and local authorities said, while Ukrainian forces reported long-range strikes on Russian logistics positions in occupied Donetsk and Zaporizhzhia.

European governments have insisted that their security interests cannot be negotiated without their participation, as Washington pursues another diplomatic initiative to end a war that is approaching its fifth year.

Key points

  • Abu Dhabi talks (Nov 10-11): prisoner exchange agreed; ceasefire and territory unresolved
  • Witkoff and Kushner in Moscow on Nov 12; a meeting with Zelensky in Dublin was also planned
  • Over 850,000 Ukrainians left without power after strikes on the energy grid
  • Europe insists no deal on its security without its seat at the table
