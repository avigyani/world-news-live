India's Retail Inflation Rises to 6.09%, Above RBI's Tolerance Band

November 13, 2026 · World News Live brief · Source: CrackTarget

Retail inflation climbed to 6.09 percent in October from 5.54 percent in September, breaching the RBI's upper tolerance limit, even as stock markets gained for a third straight session and the rupee slipped to 94.04 against the dollar.

AI-generated illustration for this story.

Retail inflation based on the All India Consumer Price Index rose to 6.09 percent in October 2026 from 5.54 percent in September, according to provisional data released by the Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation on November 12. The reading sits above the Reserve Bank of India's upper tolerance limit of 6 percent.

Markets, however, ended marginally higher for a third consecutive session. The BSE Sensex rose 31.61 points to 81,911.01 and the NSE Nifty 50 gained 44.30 points to 25,299.35, while the rupee fell 33 paise to close at 94.04 against the US dollar.

On the fiscal side, net direct tax collections for 2026-27 rose 11.76 percent year-on-year to about Rs 14.42 lakh crore between April 1 and November 10, Income Tax Department data showed.

Key points

  • CPI inflation 6.09% in October, up from 5.54% in September
  • Sensex 81,911.01; Nifty 25,299.35; rupee at 94.04 per dollar
  • Net direct tax collections up 11.76% to Rs 14.42 lakh crore
