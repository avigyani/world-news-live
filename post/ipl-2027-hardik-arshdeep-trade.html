IPL 2027's Biggest Trade: Hardik Pandya to Punjab, Arshdeep Singh to Mumbai

November 13, 2026 · World News Live brief · Source: Mid-Day

Mumbai Indians traded former captain Hardik Pandya to Punjab Kings for Rs 18 crore in a swap with Arshdeep Singh, the biggest player trade in IPL history, ahead of the November 17 retention deadline.

AI-generated illustration for this story.

Mumbai Indians have traded former captain Hardik Pandya to Punjab Kings for Rs 18 crore, swapping him with fast bowler Arshdeep Singh in what is being described as the biggest trade in the tournament's history.

The deal strengthens Mumbai's fast-bowling unit with India's leading T20I wicket-taker, while Punjab bring in a proven captain and all-rounder ahead of the IPL 2027 season.

The trade lands days before the retention deadline of November 17, with the IPL 2027 auction scheduled for December 16 in Goa. Seven Bangladesh players, led by Mustafizur Rahman and including Taskin Ahmed and Shoriful Islam, have registered for the auction.

Key points

  • Hardik Pandya to Punjab Kings for Rs 18 crore; Arshdeep Singh to Mumbai Indians
  • Described as the biggest trade in IPL history
  • Retention deadline Nov 17; auction Dec 16 in Goa
  • Seven Bangladesh players register for the auction
