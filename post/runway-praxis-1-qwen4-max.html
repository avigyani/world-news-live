Runway Opens Its Robot Brain to Everyone, Alibaba Previews Qwen4-Max

November 13, 2026 · World News Live brief · Source: SXF Brief

Runway released the weights and training code for Praxis-1, its 27-billion-parameter robot action model, while Alibaba Cloud opened a time-limited preview of its new flagship multimodal model Qwen4-Max.

AI-generated illustration for this story.

Runway made its Praxis-1 robot model generally downloadable on November 11, releasing the weights and supporting fine-tuning software for the video-trained world-action model under the Apache 2.0 licence. The 27-billion-parameter model scored 46.2 percent success on the RoboLab-120 benchmark, the company reported.

Opening the weights lets robot builders adapt a model trained on video to their own machines and tasks, rather than starting from scratch or depending on a closed system.

On the same day, Alibaba Cloud began a time-limited preview of Qwen4-Max, a flagship multimodal model that handles text, image and video understanding alongside reasoning and tool calls. Access is Token Plan-only, and the preview is scheduled to retire on November 25.

Key points

  • Praxis-1: 27B parameters, Apache 2.0, 46.2% on RoboLab-120
  • Qwen4-Max-Preview: multimodal reasoning and tool use, Token Plan-only
  • Qwen4-Max preview retires November 25
