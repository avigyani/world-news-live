Consumer Watchdog Fines Ola Rs 10 Lakh Over Pre-Ride 'Tip' Feature, Calls It a Dark Pattern

November 14, 2026 · World News Live brief · Source: Mathrubhumi

The consumer protection authority has fined Ola Rs 10 lakh over a pre-ride 'tip' feature it called a dark pattern.

Photo: Thomas R Machnitzki / Wikimedia Commons (CC BY 3.0).

The Central Consumer Protection Authority has imposed a penalty of Rs 10 lakh on ANI Technologies, which operates the ride-hailing platform Ola, for soliciting extra payments from customers before their trips were completed.

The authority objected to a pre-ride 'tip' feature that asked riders to pay more before a trip began, calling the practice a dark pattern — a design that nudges users into choices they might not otherwise make. For riders, the practical effect was simple: the app asked for money before the ride had even started.

The order follows the authority's 2023 guidelines on dark patterns, which listed practices such as false urgency, hidden charges and disguised advertising as unfair. Regulators have since been treating such interface tricks as consumer-protection issues rather than harmless design choices.

The fine is a reminder to app makers that features which pressure users into paying extra can carry a cost of their own, even when the amounts involved look small on any single ride.

Key points

  • CCPA fines Ola Rs 10 lakh
  • Pre-ride 'tip' feature asked for extra payment before trips
  • Authority called it a dark pattern
  • Penalty imposed on ANI Technologies, Ola's operator
