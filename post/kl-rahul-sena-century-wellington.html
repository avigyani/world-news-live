Key points

  • KL Rahul is the first Indian opener to score Test hundreds in all four SENA countries.
  • His 13th Test hundred took India to 311 for 2 in reply to New Zealand's 368.
  • Jaiswal's 85 and Rahul's 130-run opening stand ended India's 32-year wait for a century opening partnership in New Zealand.
  • Devdutt Padikkal added 80 in an unbroken 165-run stand with Rahul.
