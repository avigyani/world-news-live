YouTube Trending Today: GTA 6, Maatrubhumi Trailer and the Nvidia Story Dominate

November 13, 2026 · World News Live brief · Source: TrendReport

GTA 6 pre-load news topped YouTube's worldwide charts, the Maatrubhumi trailer was the most-viewed video in the US, and reports of an Nvidia board investigation drove the day's biggest news discussion on the platform.

AI-generated illustration for this story.

The most widely listed video on YouTube worldwide on November 12 was Rockstar Games' 'Grand Theft Auto VI: Pre-Load Now', appearing on the most-popular lists of 45 of the 48 countries tracked by TrendReport, with 9.8 million views. A PlayStation features trailer for the same game appeared in 43 countries.

The most-viewed video on YouTube's US trending list was the trailer for Salman Khan's Maatrubhumi, with 32.3 million views, listed in 20 countries. In India, the top video was the 'FIRE' glimpse from Vetri Maaran's Arasan, starring Silambarasan TR, with 6.3 million views.

The most-discussed news subject on YouTube, according to TrendReport's story tracking, concerned reports of an Nvidia board investigation involving its chief executive, with 255 channels posting 385 videos totalling 54.3 million views. TrendReport cites BBC News reporting on the investigation. In music, LISA's 'Tarantino' and the song 'Tu Mil Jaaye' from Shah Rukh Khan's King drew 77.5 million and 75.2 million views respectively.

Key points

  • GTA VI pre-load video listed in 45 countries, 9.8M views
  • Maatrubhumi trailer: 32.3M views, most-viewed on US trending
  • India's No.1: Arasan 'FIRE' glimpse, 6.3M views
  • Nvidia investigation story: 255 channels, 54.3M views
