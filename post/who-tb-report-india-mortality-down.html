India's TB Deaths Fall 38% Since 2015, Ahead of the Global Decline, WHO Report Finds

November 14, 2026 · World News Live brief · Source: FreeJobAlert

India's tuberculosis mortality has fallen 38% since 2015, ahead of the global decline of 35%, the WHO's Global Tuberculosis Report 2026 found.

AI-generated illustration for this story.

India's tuberculosis mortality has fallen by 38 per cent since 2015, ahead of the global decline of 35 per cent over the same period, the World Health Organization's Global Tuberculosis Report 2026 recorded.

Tuberculosis spreads through the air and mainly affects the lungs. It is curable with a months-long course of antibiotics, but late diagnosis and interrupted treatment remain common, especially where health services are hard to reach.

India's faster-than-average decline puts it ahead of the global pace, though the country still carries one of the world's largest TB burdens, and health officials say sustained detection and treatment will be needed to keep the trend going.

Health officials say the decline shows that detection and treatment programmes are working, but warn that the gains will need steady funding and follow-up to hold.

Key points

  • India's TB mortality down 38% since 2015
  • Global fall over the same period was 35%
  • Findings from the WHO Global Tuberculosis Report 2026
  • India still carries one of the world's largest TB burdens
