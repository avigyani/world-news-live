AIMIM and Azad Samaj Party Join Hands for 2027 Uttar Pradesh Polls

November 13, 2026 · World News Live brief · Source: The Hindu

Asaduddin Owaisi's AIMIM and Chandrashekhar Azad's Azad Samaj Party (Kanshiram) have announced an alliance for the 2027 Uttar Pradesh Assembly elections, with Azad set to lead the front.

AI-generated illustration for this story.

The All India Majlis-e-Ittehadul Muslimeen (AIMIM) and the Azad Samaj Party (Kanshiram) announced on Wednesday that they will contest the 2027 Uttar Pradesh Assembly elections together. Chandrashekhar Azad, who heads the Azad Samaj Party, will lead the alliance.

The two parties said the alliance aims to bring together Dalit and Muslim voters, two large voting blocs that both parties have courted separately in the past. Seat-sharing details have not been finalised yet, and talks between the two sides are expected to continue.

Neither party won a seat in the 2022 Uttar Pradesh Assembly elections. The Azad Samaj Party contested 52 seats and the AIMIM contested 95, but both drew a blank. The alliance is a fresh attempt to convert their combined support into seats in India's most populous state.

Uttar Pradesh sends 80 members to the Lok Sabha and has 403 Assembly seats, making it the most politically significant state in the country. The 2027 election is still months away, but alliances are already taking shape.

Key points

  • AIMIM and Azad Samaj Party (Kanshiram) announced an alliance on 12 November for the 2027 UP polls.
  • Chandrashekhar Azad will lead the alliance; seat-sharing talks are ongoing.
  • Both parties won no seats in 2022 — ASP(K) contested 52 seats, AIMIM 95.
  • The alliance targets a Dalit-Muslim voter consolidation in India's largest state.
