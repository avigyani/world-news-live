Nvidia Chief Jensen Huang Resigns After 33 Years; CFO Colette Kress Takes Over as Interim CEO

November 13, 2026 · World News Live brief · Source: The Indian Express

The chipmaker's board announced the resignation on Thursday, ending Huang's three-decade run at the top of the world's most valuable AI company, as shares slid.

AI-generated illustration for this story.

Nvidia said on Thursday that Jensen Huang has resigned as president, chief executive and board member, effective immediately. Chief financial officer Colette Kress will serve as interim chief executive while the board searches for a permanent successor from inside and outside the company.

Huang co-founded Nvidia in 1993 and led it for 33 years, turning a graphics-chip maker into the engine of the artificial intelligence boom and one of the world's most valuable companies. The board also named longtime director Harvey C. Jones as its first-ever chair.

The departure follows days of intense scrutiny. Reports said Huang had two extramarital relationships, including one with a businesswoman US intelligence officials suspect of links to Chinese intelligence, a claim Beijing denies. Huang admitted on Sunday to an extramarital affair, without naming the other person, and apologised to his family and colleagues.

Investors reacted nervously, with Nvidia shares falling sharply in Thursday trading. The company said its third-quarter results will still be reported on 17 November as scheduled.

Key points

  • Huang resigned with immediate effect after 33 years as CEO
  • CFO Colette Kress named interim CEO; board to consider internal and external candidates
  • Harvey C. Jones appointed Nvidia's first-ever board chair
  • Shares fell sharply as investors weighed the sudden leadership change
