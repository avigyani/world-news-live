COP31 Leaders Warn Wars Must Not Eclipse Climate Action

November 13, 2026 · World News Live brief · Source: RaahUPSC Daily Brief

On the third day of the COP31 summit in Antalya, the leaders of Brazil and Turkey said wars and trade disputes must not distract the world from the fight against global warming.

AI-generated illustration for this story.

Leaders at the COP31 climate summit in Antalya, Turkey, warned that wars and trade disputes must not eclipse climate action, as the November 9-20 talks reached their third day. The leaders of Brazil and Turkey both pressed the point, with UN Secretary-General Antonio Guterres attacking the influence of the fossil fuel lobby.

Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian said he would raise concerns over the environmental damage caused by US and Israeli military actions at the summit.

Turkey holds the COP31 presidency, with Australia as president of negotiations, and the Paris Agreement's 1.5-degree Celsius limit remains the summit's central benchmark. The Himalayas were kept off the formal agenda, according to summit reports.

Key points

  • COP31 runs Nov 9-20 in Antalya, Turkey
  • Brazil and Turkey: wars must not distract from warming fight
  • Iran to raise environmental damage from military actions
  • 1.5°C Paris limit remains the central benchmark
