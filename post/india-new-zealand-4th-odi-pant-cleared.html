India Eye Series Win in 4th ODI as Pant Gets Fitness Clearance for Tests

November 13, 2026 · World News Live brief · Source: Sunday Guardian

India need one more win to seal the five-match ODI series against New Zealand in Friday's fourth match at Bay Oval, while Rishabh Pant has been cleared fit and is on his way to join the Test squad.

AI-generated illustration for this story.

India take on New Zealand in the fourth ODI at Bay Oval, Mount Maunganui, on Friday, November 13, holding a 2-0 lead in the five-match series. Rain-affected wins in Auckland and Wellington were followed by a washed-out third match in Hamilton, so one more victory under stand-in captain Shreyas Iyer seals the series.

The build-up featured a light-hearted football challenge between Ishan Kishan and Mohammed Siraj at training, shared by the BCCI, which Kishan eventually won with a strike through a narrow gap between advertising boards.

Away from the ODI squad, wicketkeeper-batter Rishabh Pant has received fitness clearance from the BCCI medical team and left for New Zealand on Thursday, joining the Indian Test squad in Wellington on Friday. New Zealand, meanwhile, added uncapped pacer Brett Randell to their squad for the remaining two ODIs.

Elsewhere, Australia and England renew their rivalry in a three-match ODI series starting Friday at Perth Stadium, with captains Mitchell Marsh and Harry Brook unveiling the trophy on Thursday.

Key points

  • 4th ODI: Friday, Nov 13, Bay Oval; India lead 2-0, one win seals the series
  • Pant cleared fit, joins Test squad in Wellington on Friday
  • New Zealand add pacer Brett Randell for the last two ODIs
  • Australia vs England ODI series begins Friday in Perth
