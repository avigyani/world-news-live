Iran Warns US Bases Beyond the Gulf Could Be Targeted as Tankers Burn in Hormuz

November 13, 2026 · World News Live brief · Source: Gulf News

Iran's top security official warned that American bases stretching from the Middle East to Europe could be hit if US strikes resume, as two tankers caught fire in the Strait of Hormuz and Saudi Arabia reported fresh interceptions.

AI-generated illustration for this story.

Iran has warned that United States military installations stretching from the Middle East to Europe could become targets if Washington launches another military campaign against the Islamic Republic. The warning came from Mohsen Rezaei, secretary of Iran's security council and a former commander of the Revolutionary Guards, in a post on X on Wednesday.

In the Strait of Hormuz, Iran's Revolutionary Guards said two tankers carrying more than four million barrels of oil caught fire after what it called an accident on an unauthorised route. Tehran has said the strait will remain shut until its conditions are met, keeping pressure on global energy flows.

US President Donald Trump, writing on Truth Social on Thursday, said Washington does not need an agreement with Tehran, and said he was directing Vice President JD Vance to concentrate on domestic anti-fraud efforts rather than negotiations to end the conflict.

Saudi Arabia's coalition fighting Yemen's Houthi movement said it intercepted drones over the Riyadh region and eastern Saudi Arabia on Thursday. The UAE's airspace remains open, though airlines continue to adjust services and Iranian-operated flights to the UAE remain suspended.

Key points

  • Rezaei: European bases used for US attacks would also be targeted
  • Two tankers on fire in Hormuz; Iran says strait stays shut
  • Trump rules out a deal with Tehran for now
  • Saudi Arabia intercepts drones; UAE flights disrupted but airspace open
