Key points

  • New Zealand bowled out for 368 in their first innings at the Basin Reserve, Wellington.
  • Henry Nicholls carries his bat for an unbeaten 175 off 335 balls after resuming on 113.
  • Glenn Phillips hits 118 in a 216-run sixth-wicket stand after New Zealand slipped to 70 for 5.
  • Jasprit Bumrah takes four wickets and Akash Deep three; the second Test starts on 27 November.
