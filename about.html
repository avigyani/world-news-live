About World News Live

World News Live is a daily news briefing site. Every morning, we read the day's biggest stories from mainstream outlets around the world and rewrite each one as a short, original brief in our own words.

What we cover

Geopolitics, politics, technology, science, health, climate, business, sports, entertainment, cinema, world affairs and India — with priority on the stories readers are searching for each day.

Our method

  • We read reporting from established outlets such as Al Jazeera, CNN, BBC, Deccan Herald, The New York Times and The Quint.
  • We write every brief ourselves, in our own words. We never copy article text or photographs.
  • Every brief links to the original publisher so readers can read the full story at the source.

Corrections

If you spot an error in any brief, please contact us and we will correct it promptly.
